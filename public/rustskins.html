Questions

Before you ask

Where do these item names come from?
From the public RUST item database that the Rust Console server documentation points at for item names, display names and stack sizes. Entries marked as added by Spinback are well-known items with no documented stack size and are labelled as such in the detail sheet, so you can check them on your server before you rely on them.
What is inside a pack?
Open any pack on the wall and you get what its store page lists: the description, a gallery of the product's own images, and the individual skins inside it - Arctic Hazmat Suit, Arctic Assault Rifle and the Tomaha Snowmobile Skin, for example. Switch to the "Every individual skin" tab to see all of them side by side, or search for one by name.
Where does this catalogue come from?
Every entry was read from the official Rust Console Edition store at rust.double11.com/store in November 2026: the product name, the product's own image and its store page. Nothing here is invented - the fields that store does not publish (price, rarity, release date) are left blank rather than filled with a guess. Names and images belong to Double Eleven and Facepunch; Spinback RCE is not affiliated with them and sells nothing.
Can I use these items to build a kit right now?
Yes. Open the Kit Studio, pick a preset, choose your server multiplier, then add anything from this catalogue. Your kits are saved in your own browser, so no account is needed and nothing is uploaded.
Do the skins cost anything here?
No. This site does not sell skins and never takes a cut. Skins you own in Rust Console Edition are yours; this page is only a way to find and look things up.
