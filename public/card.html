Make a Christmas card

Type a name, a message and a signature, and the card below redraws as you go. It is drawn on a canvas inside this tab: nothing you type is sent anywhere, stored, or seen by the bot.

The download is a PNG of exactly what you see. Snow keeps falling while the tab is open, and stops the moment you ask your device for less motion.

What this page is, plainly

  • It is a page on this website, not a feature of the bot. Nothing in Discord changed because of it.
  • It makes no network request with what you type: the card is canvas drawing in your browser, and the download comes from that canvas.
  • It stores nothing. Close the tab and the card is gone unless you downloaded it.
