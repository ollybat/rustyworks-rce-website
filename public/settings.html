Settings

This page is about the website, not your server. Here you choose how Spinback looks and see exactly what it keeps in this browser. Your server, its roles, its log channels and its RCON connection are all set up in Discord with /setup — nothing about them is configured or stored on this site.

What you can change here

  • The theme, motion and seasonal touches, saved on this device.
  • What this browser is holding, with a button to clear each one.
  • Nothing else: no account, no sign-in, nothing to configure.

Where server settings live

  • /setup in Discord — staff roles, log channels and your Rust RCON connection.
  • /kits — emote kits, binds and cooldowns.
  • /user — player links, the roster and transfers.

Where to get help

Spinback is run from Discord, so that is where every setting, panel and fix lives.

Set it up or change it

  • /setup — staff roles, log channels and your Rust RCON connection, with a panel for each step.
  • /kits — kit list, binds, cooldowns and giving a kit.
  • /user — link a player, unlink, transfer or read the roster.
  • /ping and /help — what is running and what needs doing.
