About Spinback RCE

Spinback RCE is a free Discord bot for Rust Console Edition servers. This website is its shop window and its reference shelf: what the bot does, every command it registers, and the live state of the service behind it.

What the bot is for

A Rust server admin's day is a lot of small console chores and a lot of small player questions. The bot takes both: it talks to your server over RCON for the chores, keeps its own records of who is linked to whom, and answers players where they already are - inside Discord.

It runs on the server's own RCON connection, which you set up once with /setup and which it tests before saving. Nothing about your server is stored anywhere else, and none of it is published here.

What this website is, and is not

  • It is a helper: it explains the bot, lists its commands, and shows its public status.
  • It is read-only. It has no accounts, no logins and no way for a visitor to change anything about your server.
  • It will not guess. Where the bot or its source has not stated something - a price, a date, a description - the page says so instead of filling it in.
  • Where it shows numbers, it names where they came from and how old they are.
