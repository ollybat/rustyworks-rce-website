Continue with Discord

Checking your session...

You will land on your servers page. Discord is asked for your name, your avatar and the servers you are in. It cannot read your messages, post anything, or see your email.

What you get

  • Servers - every server you can manage, and whether Spinback is in it
  • Profile - the account you signed in with, your roles and your access
  • Settings - theme, seasonal look, font, text size and calm mode
  • Dashboard - the control panel, for the server owner and staff

Inside Discord the bot has one command, /dashboard, and only the server owner sees it. Everything above works from the website alone.

If something goes wrong

Told the dashboard is owner-only? You signed in with a different Discord account than the one that owns the server - sign out and try again. Nothing else here needs staff access.

Your session lasts seven days and lives in this browser, so signing out on a shared computer really does end it.
