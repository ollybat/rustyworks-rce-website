Sign in to your Dashboard

The dashboard controls one Discord server — the one this bot runs in. Signing in with Discord is how the site checks that you are the owner of it, and nobody else.

Continue with Discord

Checking your session…

You will land on your dashboard. The site asks Discord for two things only — identify (your username and avatar) and guilds (the servers you own). It cannot read messages, post anything, or see your email.

Who can get in

  • Only the owner of the server the bot runs in — one server, one owner
  • Any other Discord account is refused, whatever they sign in with
  • The session lasts 7 days, then you sign in again
  • Signing out clears it on this device straight away

Inside Discord, the bot has a single command — /dashboard. Only the owner can see it, and it answers privately with a link back here.

Check bot status →

Trouble signing in?

If you are told the dashboard is owner-only, you signed in with a different Discord account than the one that owns the server — sign out and try again. If the browser blocks the round trip to Discord, make sure cookies are allowed for this site, then start again. Anything else, ask in the support server.
